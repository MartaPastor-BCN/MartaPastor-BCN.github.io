About

I partner with Sales across EMEA from discovery to go-live, helping enterprise advertisers, agencies and partners turn ad tech and AI into solutions that launch, and helping bring new offerings to market through pilots and seller enablement.

I joined Microsoft in a commercial role with no technical background, just a persistent need to understand the "why". Nine months later I moved into technical pre-sales. Along the way I learned that the fastest way to solve a recurring customer problem is often to build the tool yourself, together with the people who will use it.

Before Microsoft, I was the second hire on the founding team of MediaMarkt Iberia's retail media business. Together with an incredible team, we grew it from €0 to €15M.

Selected work

Solution design · Automation

A planning tool that cut campaign setup from 72 minutes to 3

Problem
Setting up a media plan and deal was slow, manual and repeated across many tools, which delayed proposals for sellers and clients.
What I did
Mapped the workflow, identified the bottleneck and defined the requirements, APIs and data model for a planning tool, working with Product and Engineering.
Result
Campaign setup dropped from ~72 to ~3 minutes, and the tool was rolled out to new EMEA markets.
AI agents · Operations

An AI agent for media-booking operations

Problem
Account teams lost time on repetitive deal activation steps and workflow checks.
What I did
Built an AI agent based on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) that automates deal activation and workflow checks, during a Microsoft hackathon.
Result
~40% faster booking SLAs in pilot. Ops Excellence Award, Microsoft Hackathon 2025.
Go-to-market · Enablement

Taking new video and curated offerings to market

Problem
New CTV, online video and curated deal offerings needed a repeatable way to be sold, set up and delivered across EMEA.
What I did
Contributed to a brand pilot's sales motion and pricing approach, delivered package-level proposals, wrote runbooks and guides, and trained sellers, Customer Success teams and agency partners.
Result
Repeatable workflows and enablement materials used by sellers and Customer Success teams across EMEA markets.
